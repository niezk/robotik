# Get your brand verified and your profiles official in OrcaSlicer

Two independent programs

## Choose the program that matches your goal

Each program has its own meaning, review focus, and application. Vendors may apply for one program or submit separate applications for both.

Program 01

### OrcaCloud account verification

For vendors that want their OrcaCloud account recognized as an authorized vendor presence for community-facing profile and bundle activity.

* Best for building brand trust in OrcaCloud.
* OrcaSlicer reviews identity and authorization.
* Creates a clear verified account signal for OrcaCloud users.

Status meaning

Verified vendor account identity in OrcaCloud, shown through a recognizable account badge and official label.

### Official profiles in OrcaSlicer with OTA support

For vendors that want official printer or filament profiles available directly in OrcaSlicer and updateable through OTA profile delivery.

* Best for vendor-managed official profiles.
* OTA support helps profile updates reach users without a full app release.
* Creates a clear place for users to find official vendor profile updates.

Status meaning

Official vendor profile channel in OrcaSlicer with OTA profile support.

Program comparison

## Two programs, two different purposes

Account verification confirms vendor identity in OrcaCloud. The official profiles program creates a vendor-managed official profile channel in OrcaSlicer with OTA updates.

| Program                            | Best for                                                                      | What you get                                                            | Where it appears                  | Review focus                                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| OrcaCloud account verification     | Vendors sharing profiles and bundles with the community through OrcaCloud.    | A verified badge and official label on your OrcaCloud account.          | OrcaCloud (cloud.orcaslicer.com). | Company identity and representative authorization.           |
| Official profiles with OTA support | Vendors maintaining official printer or filament profiles for their products. | An official vendor profile channel with OTA updates delivered to users. | Inside the OrcaSlicer app.        | Identity, authorization, profile scope, and update workflow. |

Application flow

## From application to approval in four steps

01

### Select a program

Choose the program that matches your goal — account verification, official profiles, or both.

02

### Complete and submit

Fill in the application and email it to partners@orcaslicer.com from your company mailbox.

03

### Review

OrcaSlicer reviews your company identity, representative authorization, and program details, and follows up by email.

04

### Go live

On approval, your verified badge or official profile channel is activated and visible to users.

Status meaning

## What your status tells users — and what it does for you

Both statuses are reviewed, free, and never sold — which is exactly why users learn to trust them.

### OrcaCloud account verification

* Your account stands out from lookalikes — the verified badge and official label confirm the account is really yours.
* Profiles and bundles you share carry your brand's credibility, so they travel further in the community.
* Impersonator and copycat accounts become easy for users to spot and avoid.
* Verification confirms identity — it is not certification, product endorsement, a print-quality or support guarantee, ranking, or paid placement.

### Official profiles with OTA support

* Users printing on your hardware or filament get profiles you tuned, straight inside OrcaSlicer.
* Ship profile improvements over the air — no waiting for an app release or asking users to reinstall.
* Fewer support tickets from outdated or mismatched settings, because users always see your latest recommendations.
* Official status identifies the source — it is not certification, product endorsement, a print-quality or support guarantee, ranking, or paid placement.

FAQ

## Common questions for vendors

<details>

<summary>Do the programs need to be completed in order?</summary>

No. The programs are independent. A vendor may apply for the program that fits their goal, and approval in one program does not automatically grant the other.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does OrcaCloud account verification mean?</summary>

It means OrcaSlicer has reviewed vendor identity and authorization for the submitted OrcaCloud account, allowing the account to appear with a verified identity signal.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do official profiles help users?</summary>

Official profiles give users a recognizable vendor profile channel in OrcaSlicer, with OTA support for profile updates after approval.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do the programs cost anything?</summary>

No. Both programs are free of charge. Verification and official profile status are granted through review and are never sold, sponsored, or offered as paid placement.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can a vendor apply for both programs?</summary>

Yes. A vendor may submit separate applications for both programs when both account verification and official profile participation are relevant.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How long does review take?</summary>

Applications are reviewed manually and we follow up by email either way, typically within two weeks. Reviews may take longer when identity or authorization needs additional confirmation.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can a status be suspended or revoked?</summary>

Yes. Status reflects a confirmed identity and scope. If that can no longer be confirmed — for example the brand changes ownership, account control is lost, or program acknowledgements are no longer met — OrcaSlicer may suspend or revoke the status. Vendors should notify [partners@orcaslicer.com](mailto:partners@orcaslicer.com) of material changes.

</details>

## Ready to start?

Pick the application that matches the program you want to pursue. To join both programs, submit a separate application for each. Questions before applying? Email [partners@orcaslicer.com](mailto:partners@orcaslicer.com).
